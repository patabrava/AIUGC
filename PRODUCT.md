# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Lippe Lift Studio editors and operators producing short-form UGC for TikTok and Instagram. They work through script review, scene approval, paid video generation, quality checks, delivery, and publishing, and need to understand the current gate and next action without interpreting backend state codes.

## Product Purpose

Provide a deterministic, recoverable production workflow for character-consistent vertical video. Success means an operator can move through each approval and production stage in order, see which work is automatic or paid, recover failed work without duplicate charges, and publish a verified delivery.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and practical. The interface should feel calm during long-running provider work and precise at approval or spending decisions.

## Anti-references

Avoid undifferentiated dashboard pages that expose every stage at once, progress indicators detached from the work actually running, hidden automatic transitions, and decorative status UI that does not identify the next operator action.

## Design Principles

- Make the next required action unmistakable.
- Derive progress from persisted production truth.
- Separate operator gates from automatic work.
- Preserve evidence and recovery paths after every paid action.
- Keep advanced diagnostics available without competing with the primary flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, semantic structure, visible focus states, ARIA labels and live regions, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion support. Status must never rely on color alone.
