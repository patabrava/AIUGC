## Intent

Create two distinct, silent, eight-second vertical test videos whose only purpose is proving that the publishing integration attaches each platform's native AI-generated-content disclosure. Both videos must look unmistakably synthetic and carry conspicuous on-frame test copy so they cannot be confused with ordinary marketing content.

## Locked outputs

- Two MP4 files, each exactly eight seconds at 1080×1920 and 30 fps.
- Variant 1 uses a dark cobalt/cyan technical signal language.
- Variant 2 uses a warm vermilion/cream verification-stamp language.
- Both show “AI-GENERATED”, “DISCLOSURE TEST”, and a distinct “TEST 1” or “TEST 2” identifier.
- No narration, music, people, product claims, logos, or external assets.

## Execution

Use deterministic, asset-free kinetic typography. Render immediately after the HyperFrames check and proof snapshots pass; the user's request to create and post the videos is explicit render approval.
